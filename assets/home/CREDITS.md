# Home furniture credits

The new furniture shapes were made for Delta City in Blender. They use original geometry, with a small set of CC0 photo textures packed into the `.glb` files from Poly Haven:

- [Sofa 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/sofa_02) — fabric colour texture.
- [Oak Veneer 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01) — wood texture.
- Linen White — fabric texture included in the existing Delta City starter bed asset.
- [Electric Stove](https://polyhaven.com/a/electric_stove) — appliance texture included in the existing Delta City starter cooker asset.

Poly Haven states that its models and textures are CC0: [Poly Haven licence](https://polyhaven.com/license).

Existing models retained from the starter set:

- `living-room/sofa_3-seater_charcoal.glb` — Poly Haven Sofa 02, CC0.
- `kitchen/cooker_electric_starter.glb` — Poly Haven Electric Stove, CC0.
- `dining-other/dining_chair_starter.glb` — Poly Haven Painted Wooden Chair 01, CC0.
- `dining-other/dining_table_starter.glb` — Poly Haven Dining Table, CC0.
- Other starter models were previously made for Delta City; their materials use CC0 Poly Haven textures.

No CC-BY assets are included, so there are no attribution-only licence obligations.
